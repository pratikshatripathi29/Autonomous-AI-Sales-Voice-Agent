# NovaCloud AI Frequently Asked Questions

## Setup & Onboarding
Most teams go live in under 48 hours. Upload your product docs, connect your CRM webhook, and configure your business hours. No engineering team is required for the Starter or Growth plans.

## Trial & Cancellation
We offer a 14-day free trial with 100 free voice minutes and no credit card required. All monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with zero cancellation fees.

## Security & Compliance
All call transcripts and lead records are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We support role-based access controls and SOC 2 Type II compliant hosting.

## Support & Demo Availability
Live demos are available Monday through Friday between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. For custom enterprise integrations, our solutions engineering team joins the onboarding call.
