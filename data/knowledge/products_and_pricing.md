# NovaCloud AI Products & Pricing

## AI Voice Receptionist
The NovaCloud AI Voice Receptionist answers inbound customer calls 24/7 in English, Hindi, and Punjabi. It qualifies prospects, answers product FAQs, and routes urgent issues to human reps.
- Starter Plan: $49/month for up to 500 voice minutes and 1 phone number.
- Growth Plan: $149/month for up to 2,500 voice minutes, CRM webhook sync, and multi-language support.
- Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing starting at $499/month with dedicated SLA, custom voice cloning, and unlimited seats.

## Automated Lead Qualifier
Scores inbound leads from 0 to 100 based on budget, timeline, urgency, and product fit, syncing qualified leads directly to HubSpot, Salesforce, or webhook CRMs.

## Smart Demo Scheduler
Checks real-time calendar availability and books 30-minute product demos automatically during the call without double-booking.
